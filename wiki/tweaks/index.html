Tweaks

Tweaks from Canister or an APT repository go into your tweaks library, and from there you attach them to an app.

  1. Find tweaks under Canister or in your Repos.
  2. Download them into Tweaks.
  3. Press Attach… on the app's pane.

A .deb or .dylib you already have goes into the tweaks folder too.

Canister, Repos and Tweaks

  • Canister searches canister.me. A download by package id is checked against Canister's published SHA-256, when Canister has one.
  • Repos adds APT repositories and lists their packages. Free repositories only; repos that need a login or payment aren't supported.
  • Tweaks lists your library (~/.config/graft/tweaks/), checks it for newer published versions when you ask, and updates a tweak, re-pointing the apps that use it. Deleting one an app still uses asks first, and offers Detach and Delete. Deleted tweaks go to the Trash.

Updating an app (Update & Install on its pane, or graft --update) also updates every tweak it uses that has a newer build, once for all the apps that share it. While an update of an app runs, the Update button in Tweaks for the tweaks that app uses waits until it's done. See Updating your apps.

Shared libraries

cyan bundles ElleKit as CydiaSubstrate and adds a few common frameworks when a tweak links them, but not every shared library a tweak may link against (color or settings libraries, for example).

If a build fails with Library not loaded: @rpath/libexample.dylib, put a sandbox-compatible copy of that library in the tweaks folder and attach it to the app, as you would a .deb.

Rootless tweaks only

Tweaks built for RootHide depend on libroothide.dylib, which doesn't exist outside a jailbreak. Use the rootless build from the developer's own repository or releases.
