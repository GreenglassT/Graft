Graft Docs

Graft takes a decrypted IPA, injects .deb or .dylib tweaks into it, and delivers the result to your iPhone or iPad over USB or Wi-Fi.

User Guide

  • Getting Started: install Graft, see what it needs, and follow the five steps from setting up your Apple account to installing an app.
  • Your Apple Account: set Graft up for a free Apple ID or a paid Apple Developer account, and switch later.
  • Adding Apps: add an app from an App Store search, its App Store URL, or a name and bundle id.
  • Getting an IPA: the three ways to get the decrypted IPA a build needs.
  • App Store Downloads: sign in and set up decryption once, then download and decrypt apps your Apple ID owns or can get for free.
  • Tweaks: find tweaks on Canister or in APT repositories, and attach them to an app.
  • Building & Delivery: build an app with its tweaks, push it into LiveContainer or, with a paid account, install it as a normal app, and keep track of past builds and deliveries.
  • LiveContainer: use the LiveContainer on your device, or install and update one from Graft with a paid account, and see which one a build pushes into.

Terminal

  • Installing the CLI: put the graft command on your Mac, either from Graft.app or with pipx.
  • Quick Start: the commands for a first build from Terminal, with a free Apple ID or a paid Apple Developer account.
  • CLI Reference: the graft commands, grouped by what they do.
  • Scripting & Environment: drive graft from a script with --json, and see which environment variables it reads, including the ones for a proxy.

Reference

  • Configuration & Files: what config.yaml holds, and where Graft keeps its files.
  • What Graft Downloads: when Graft goes online and what it downloads, including the one-time downloads on the first App Store action.
  • What Graft Connects To: every host Graft and its tools contact, why, when, and what is sent.
  • Updating Graft: how to move to a new version of Graft.app.
  • Upgrading from 1.x: what happens to your config, pipx graft, Homebrew tools and App Store sign-in, and how to go back to 1.1.
  • Troubleshooting: common symptoms and error messages, and their likely cause.
  • Removing Graft: what to delete to remove Graft, and what it made outside the app.
  • Building from Source: how to build Graft.app and run its tests from the repository.
