Graft Docs

Graft is a Mac app that finds an iOS app, downloads and decrypts it, adds your tweaks, and sends it to your iPhone or iPad. It works with a free Apple ID.

User Guide

  • Getting Started: install Graft and check what it needs, with a page for each of the five steps to installing an app.
  • Your Apple Account: Graft works with a free Apple ID or a paid Apple Developer account, and Settings can switch between the two later.
  • Adding Apps: add an app by searching the App Store, or from its App Store URL or a name and bundle id.
  • Getting an IPA: the three ways to get the decrypted IPA a build needs.
  • App Store Downloads: once you've signed in and set up decryption, Graft downloads and decrypts the apps your Apple ID owns or can get for free.
  • Tweaks: tweaks from Canister or an APT repository go into your library, and you attach them to an app from there.
  • Building & Delivery: building an app with its tweaks and pushing it into LiveContainer, or with a paid account installing it as a normal app.
  • LiveContainer: the LiveContainer already on your device, or one Graft installs and updates with a paid account.

Terminal

  • Installing the CLI: put the graft command on your Mac, either from Graft.app or with pipx.
  • Quick Start: the commands for a first build from Terminal, with a free Apple ID or a paid Apple Developer account.
  • CLI Reference: the graft commands, grouped by what they do.
  • Scripting & Environment: scripts drive graft with --json, and the environment variables it reads include the ones for a proxy.

Reference

  • Configuration & Files: Graft stores its settings and each app's options in config.yaml, and its other files in folders of its own.
  • What Graft Downloads: when Graft goes online and what it downloads, including the one-time downloads on the first App Store action.
  • What Graft Connects To: every host Graft and its tools contact, why, when, and what is sent.
  • Updating Graft: Graft tells you when a newer version is out, and you install it by hand from GitHub Releases.
  • Changelog: what changed in each release of Graft, newest first.
  • Troubleshooting: common symptoms and error messages, and their likely cause.
  • Removing Graft: what to delete to remove Graft, and what it made outside the app.
  • Building from Source: you can build Graft.app from the repository with xcodegen and Xcode, and run its tests there.
