What Graft Downloads

When Graft goes online and what it downloads, including the one-time downloads on the first App Store action.

Graft has no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting. Graft.app makes no connections of its own: its CLI does, for something you asked for or a pane you opened; to look up the icon of an app that has none yet; once a day to ask GitHub whether a newer Graft is out, and to download it; and once a day to check whether your apps and tweaks have updates. Settings › Updates turns off the daily checks and the download. Device names and UDIDs never leave your Mac.

The first App Store action on a Mac (usually the first sign-in) PyPI (files.pythonhosted.org), and Apple's software CDN ipatool's Unicorn emulator (a few MB) and its App Store components (about 320 MB), once

About that first App Store action:

  • Graft.app doesn't include Unicorn. The bundled ipatool downloads Unicorn 2.1.4 from PyPI on first use, as upstream ipatool does, and checks it against the SHA-256 its source pins.
  • Both downloads are cached in ~/Library/Caches/ipatool, so they happen once, and again only for a new version.
  • Offline, that first App Store action stops with "ipatool needs to download its App Store emulator (Unicorn, from PyPI) the first time; connect to the internet and try again."

UnfairPlay has no network code, and talking to your iPhone or iPad stays on your Mac and your local network. What Graft connects to lists every host, when, and what is sent.
